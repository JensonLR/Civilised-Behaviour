A dispatch from the Society

To all members in good standing, and to the others:

The Society has been improving the world for some time, chiefly by explaining to it what it was doing wrong. The latest instalment is a game. One to four members sail to a coast that already has owners, a toll, a bridge and a very good lawyer, and are invited to civilise it.

The locals have not read the pamphlet. They have factions, long memories and ideas about who is going to be improved. Do not assume that you are the ones holding the ledger.

The Secretary, by order, and without apology

What it is

  • An expedition sandbox

    Land on a shore, take a contract, and settle it by parley, bribery, force or a very large lie. Every ending leaves a mark on the campaign.

  • One to four, together

    Co-operative play on an authoritative server. Share the crates, the blame and the bill.

  • Places that push back

    Invented countries with factions, humour and agendas of their own. The satire is aimed at institutions, never at peoples.

  • A paper that remembers

    The morning broadsheet reports what you did, in the Society's own euphemisms. It is not always flattering, and it is never corrected.
