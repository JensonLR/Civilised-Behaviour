Mature content disclosure

  • Violence: heavy, cartoonish and bloody, including injury and dismemberment.
  • Language: profanity.
  • Themes: dark satire of imperial and bureaucratic institutions, colonial-era pastiche, war and its paperwork. All peoples, places, faiths and institutions in the game are invented.
