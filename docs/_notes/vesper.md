# Vesper Gorge (region three, package C3, D-037)

Record of what was built, why it is shaped as it is, how it is tested, and what is still open. Spec: `docs/_notes/regions34.md` section 3. Home power: the Low Vesper Lamentation Guild (`choir`).

## The place
A VERTICAL region: a gorge 300 m long and about 70 m wide, banded sandstone walls 30-45 m high, a dry river bed down the floor, a plateau rim above. Palette `PALETTE.vesper` (`paletteVesper.ts`, ~33 keys): strata rust/bone/plum/buff, the Company's red and cream, the Guild's plum and silver, the Syndicate's green and gilt, lamp glow. Swatch: ground dust, wall strataRust, roof crepe, accent strataPlum, cloth glowLamp.

Plan (`packages/shared/src/vesper.ts`, pure, deterministic, allocation-free in the step; `VESPER_STATUS.stub` is `false` and `REGIONS.vesper.reachable` is `true`):
- Terrain: the floor climbs 3.5% from the Staithe Landing (z 118) to the Lower Gallery (z -96); cliff feet and heights are knot tables; strata ledges are `rise()` steps; the west bench (pegging ground, Long Cloister) and the east bench (Assay House); the east terrace for the headframe, reached by a ramp and by nothing else; the tipple ledge; the wharf basin; the head wall.
- Cliffs are TRUE walls. The slope test (`walkable()`) reads terrain only and only when grounded, so a jump climbs any heightfield: every cliff foot, the head and every terrace face also carries a buried box collider (`wallSeg("cliff", ...)`, parapets with a gap at the deck). Tests jump-and-charge every face.
- The ore trestle crosses the gorge at z -66 on piles. Its deck is tagged "wall" and its piles "pole" (the nav grid reads "bridge" as a floor and would cut the road): you can walk the deck AND under it; hidden ramp strips under both deck ends join it to the terrain.
- The rock fall is a solid plug box at z -96 across the whole width. The eleven miners are placed behind it without any collision change; nobody reaches them by walking (flood-fill test with the real `stepCharacter`, layered so the deck counts).
- Sites: landing (0,118), road (7 points), adit (0,-92), headframe (20,-66), Assay House (30,18), pegging ground (-38,-30), Long Cloister (-40,44). Stock: five crates of pit-prop timber, one keg of blasting powder, the dig point (0,-93), the blast point (0,-96.6).
- Skyline (seed 7): from `road[3]` walled 0.875, mean 19.0 deg, max 36.8, spikes 4 (bars: walled >= 0.6, mean >= 12, max >= 20); from the landing max 33.0, spikes 2 (bars: max >= 14, spikes >= 1). The spec's "spikes >= 1 (the headframe)" cannot come from the headframe: it stands 186 m from the landing, beyond the skyline's 150 m range. Rock needles on the plateau supply the spike. Kessar and Highmark do not satisfy the Vesper bars.
- View budget tightened to meshes {22,28,44,44}, triangles {120k,170k,440k,540k}; measured medium 30 meshes / 232k triangles.

## The contracts
1. `mine_rescue` "The Lower Gallery". Eleven miners behind a fall. Pressures: the AIR CLOCK (420-460 s; the Guild has estimated it, at a profit); the FOREMAN's schedule (parley `foreman`: handling charge, Variance Form stamped after 45 s, ask when it seals); TIMBER (carry a crate to the fall, three sets shore it; the face holds 25% unshored and 25% more per set); the POWDER (carry the keg to the fall, 5 s fuse); the GUILD (parley `dirge_master`: settle the bill, a vigil for +90 s of air, once told what the Guild knows an objection that stays the seal). Endings `dug_out` (forty shovel-presses behind three sets), `blasted_through`, `sealed` (the schedule ran out), `consecrated` (the air ran out or the bill was paid), plus the forced `abandoned`. A dead foreman files no seals.
2. `claim_race` "The Claim Race". Four pegs, the Syndicate's two surveyors physically on the bench and four guards at the headframe. Pressures: pegs (INTERACT), the clerk (parley `assayer`: file, joint claim, ask, tell), the Syndicate's clock (it pegs the lowest open corner from 80-100 s and then every 55 s while a surveyor stands, and files 35 s after its third), force. Endings `staked`, `jumped` (their pegs pulled by force or because the clerk marked the survey PROVISIONAL), `partnered` (a peg each, the peace kept), `outpaced`, plus `abandoned`.

Eight endings with ledger numbers (`vesperLedger.ts`), authored copy (`vesperText.ts`), a favour (`VESPER_FAVOUR = { choir: [sealed, blasted_through, consecrated] }`) and `pickVesperContract`: the Lower Gallery first (the contract test founds a dev start on it), the Claim Race first only when the Syndicate has both the influence (>= 50) and surveyors in the gorge; after that never the same twice running, weighted by the ledger. Three parley scripts in `scenarios/vesperParleyText.ts` (`ParleyScript` data; `{price}` is filled in open/round-2/flatter lines, never in deal lines).

Runner semantics that shaped the templates (from `Scenario.ts`, unchanged): `UseSpec.carry` is `crate`/`barrel`/`none` and a use with a prop consumes it only if the machine accepted the press; `onProp` reports destroyed barrels only when a wagon exists, so the keg is a `use` at the fall, not a prop event; a talk event does not say which person, so the template keys off `kind`; the cast's counts arrive as `count` events; `resolveWith` puts commit last and the first resolution wins, after which the state is frozen.

## The client view (`apps/client/src/render/world/vesper/`)
`VesperView` composes `ground` (terrain with a fragment-shader strata band patch, `vWPos` + `dFdx` flat normals, so each ledge keeps its colour), `solid` (the town and the works as one Kit-merged vertex-coloured mesh with an ink hull: Long Cloister, Assay House, yard, tents, carts, spoil, lamps, the headframe and its sheave, the trestle, the tipple, rails, pegs, the wharf), `rocks` and `scatter` (boulders, outcrops, needles, scrub, tufts, snags, reeds as instanced meshes), `cloth` (a signs-and-banners atlas with a wave shader), `dress` and the shared ambient/atmosphere uniforms.

`applyScenario(view)` DRESSES the contract (it never decides it and never touches collision): the Lower Gallery's fall is shored with up to three timber sets as the party carries them, cut into as it digs, and finally an opening (`dug_out`), a breach (`blasted_through`), a seal (`sealed`: planks, iron straps and the Company's red plate) or a Guild service (`consecrated`: plum drapes, candles, a bell). The Claim Race's four pegs fly a gilt flag when the party holds them and a green one when the Syndicate does (one instanced mesh, four slots). State is read from the published view's objectives (`timber` "(n of 3)", `dig` "(NN%)", `peg-0..3`), so it works for any client of the room. A URL dev hook (`?fall=open|dug|blasted|sealed|consecrated`, `?pegs=party,rival,none,party`) dresses stills without a room.

## How it is tested
- shared: `vesper.test.ts` (15: determinism, floor/road, true-wall slopes, anchors open, no overlaps, layered flood fill with the real step, miners sealed behind the plug, closed cliffs, trestle walked on and under, jump-and-charge on every face, nav grid < 150 ms, skyline bars and their discrimination), `scenarios/mineRescue.test.ts` (23), `scenarios/claimRace.test.ts` (21), `vesperLedger.test.ts` (12).
- server (the real `Scenario` runner on a fake host and fake Cast; no server source): `systems/MineRescue.test.ts` (23), `systems/ClaimRace.test.ts` (21), both with a 600-sequence forged-input fuzz; `rooms/vesper.room.test.ts` (4, port 2603): a dev start at Vesper, `consecrated` DRIVEN for real (INTERACT, a stranger's picks ignored, the owner's `parleyPick`), the forced `abandoned`, and all eight through `outcome:`.
- client: `render/world/vesper/vesper.test.ts` (20): budgets per preset, parts, outlines per preset, dispose, geometry finite and outward, signs Latin capitals with no real-world term, scatter determinism, `dressOf` over the real views the reducers publish, `applyScenario` state per ending, peg flags, no colour literal in the folder.
- Mutation checks (each run once, then reverted): dropped the fall plug (`vesper.test` fails 3), `dug_out` at 50% (`mineRescue.test` fails), `need` 3 -> 2 (`claimRace.test` and `ClaimRace.test` fail), `sealed` numbers copied from `consecrated` (`vesperLedger.test` fails 3), fuse 5 -> 50 s (`MineRescue.test` fails 3), first contract forced to `claim_race` (`vesper.room.test` fails 3), first timber set hidden (`vesper.test` client fails). One honest miss: removing the Guild's own minor delta from `dug_out` is NOT caught, because the central `powersAfterOutcome` moves the home power from `news.a` regardless.
