# Expedition slice: smarter enemies, horses and a wagon, hired hands, four scenarios (D-034)

Goal: the loop gets WEIGHT. Prep at the map room (loadout, hires, a mount) -> sail -> ONE of four scenarios offered by the campaign (not always the bridge) against enemies that path, take cover, miss
believably and break -> outcome mutates the campaign -> paper. Verified-by-reading against: slice.md, WorldRoom.ts, Scenario.ts, Travel.ts, Combat.ts (`hittable`, `onFrame`, aim slack 0.6 rad, `noise` hook), scenario.ts, garrison.ts,
movement.ts, campaignTypes.ts, factions.ts (`applyOutcome` RULES, `wardMemory`), newspaper.ts, kessar.ts, villagerNav.ts, Game.ts nametags (line ~560), GDD 3-5.
Process is D-033's: a frozen contract (phase 0), four packages with DISJOINT files, one integrator who alone edits shared wiring. Nobody but H runs `scripts/shot.mjs`.
Rejected: a navmesh (one static world + one collapsed bridge: a 2 m grid rebuilt in <80 ms is enough); Rapier characters for NPCs; a second predicted entity for the horse (one reconciler is hard enough: the horse is a picture of the rider's
predicted state); Rapier vehicles for the wagon (tuning and determinism cost, nothing to gain); per-package NPC loops (one `Cast` runs every NPC row, brains plug in).

## 2. Package N: Cast, navigation, utility brain, balance, plates

- `nav.ts`: `buildNavGrid(world, {bounds, cell=NAV.cell, clearance=NAV.clearance, wetY?}): NavGrid` (cached per world in a WeakMap). A cell is `open` when a body of radius `clearance` stands there under the shared rules: `resolveXZ` does not move it, the ground height is
  the walking surface (obstacle tags `bridge`/`jetty`/`weir` are floors, as in villagerNav), neighbour edges obey `CHARACTER.maxSlope*0.8` and `stepHeight`, and deep water (`wetY`, Kessar passes its bed level) is closed except the ford. `tall` marks cells whose obstacle is >= 1.1 m (blocks sight and bullets).
  `class NavQuery implements NavApi`: A* (8-neighbour, binary heap in typed arrays, generation stamps so nothing is cleared or allocated per query, `NAV.expansionCap` then the best partial path with `complete:false`), string-pulled with `los` (grid DDA), `cover` (scan <= radius for an open cell
  with a `tall` cell on the threat side within 2 cells, nearest first), `flank` (point `dist` from the target at +-70 degrees off the bearing from allies, snapped with `nearestOpen`). Rebuild on `Cast.setWorld` (bridge collapse). Budget: build < 80 ms for Kessar, median query < 0.4 ms, 0 heap growth in a 10k-query loop.
- `npcBrain.ts`: `npcThink: BrainFn` + `npcBrainNew(spec): NpcBrain`. Utility scores in [0,1], re-decided at 4 Hz staggered by `lookSeed`, +0.15 inertia to the current mode for 1.2 s (no thrash): fire = threat*token*rangeFit*(0.4+0.6*morale); advance = threat*(1-rangeFit)*morale; cover = threat*underFire*coverNear; flank = threat*(1-token)*bold*allyGap
  (two allies already frontal); retreat = (1-morale)*threat, fighting withdrawal toward `post` by `nav.path`; flee when `moraleBand` is "broken" (sprint away, latched until morale >= 55 and 25 m clear); hold = 0.15. Believable misses (all numbers in one exported `NPC_TUNING` table): reaction `0.35 + (1-skill/100)*0.9 + hash jitter 0..0.3` s after a NEW target
  (seen again after > 2 s), turning at half `MOVEMENT.turnRate` meanwhile; aim yaw error sigma `weaponBase*(1+dist/20)*(moving?1.6:1)*(1.6-skill/100)` sampled per burst via `hash3`, shrinking 25% per consecutive shot at a target that is not moving (ranging in), reset when the target moves; `aimElev` +-0.02; written to `out.aimYaw/aimElev` (slack 0.6 rad is ample);
  cadence: rifle one shot then a 0.3-1.2 s think; pistol bursts of 2 then 1.0-2.2 s; blunderbuss only inside 14 m; melee closes only with a token and pads 0.4 s. `garrison.ts` keeps `garrisonRoster` (now filling `side/group/skill/bravery/brain`, <= NPC_CAP) and drops its own `NpcSpec` (now in the contract; no duplicate export).
- `Cast.ts`: `class Cast implements CastApi`, ctor `(host: { players; spawnNpc(spec): boolean; removeNpc(key): void; stepNpc(key, cmd): void; world(): CollisionWorld; worldMs(): number; seed: number; fear(): number; brains: Partial<Record<BrainId, BrainFn>>; sendTo?; })`.   It reads `row.hp/wounds` itself for `hurtAt`. Groups: `order(group, o)` is the only way scenarios steer people. `civil` brain is inline (hostage/driver: stand, flee from fire, follow when freed). Cost: <= 2 ms per tick for 20 NPCs.

## 7. Decided rules (slice-1 loose ends)
1. **Sailing away mid-scenario COMMITS.** `enterRegion` calls `scenario.leave()` before `dispose`. Never reached the bar / site and nothing happened (phase `approach`, empty tally, not hostile) = DISMISSED, nothing is committed and nothing is lost (a misclick is not a war). A lit fuse = `sabotaged` (the bridge falls unwatched, onBridge 0). Otherwise `abandoned`, or the template's own `leave` resolution (table in section 5), with the tally and `brokePromise` so far: kills, limbs and broken promises are remembered.
2. **No trust farming.** After `paid`, `bargained`, `bribed` or `forced` the crossing is SETTLED for `SETTLED_DAYS` campaign days: `pickTemplate` does not offer it, a dev-forced entry starts resolved ("the crossing is on the books"), so re-paying is impossible. Defence in depth: `applyOutcome` scales trust gains by 0.5 when `wardMemory.repeat >= 2`. After the window the toll lapses back to `askingToll` and the crossing returns. `sabotaged` leaves a ruin (the ford); `rival_secured` and `abandoned` do not settle.
3. **Name plates are culled, not clamped** (rule in N). 4. **A scripted blast has an owner**: the lighter of the charge. The blast follows the normal rules (the owner takes the halved self rule, the garrison full damage, other party members only with friendly fire on). 5. A downed follower is NOT reaped after 30 s like the garrison (`reapNpcs` exempts side `party`): it lies there until a human or the surgeon revives it, and is marked `wounded` in the roster if the expedition ends first.
