# The Reapers' Strike (D-042, Highmark's second contract; the GDD's "labour dispute")

Highmark has had one contract since D-036, so every visit there told the same story. The GDD's v1 list wants a labour dispute and the region already holds every
piece it needs: the Thornfield Reapers (the minor power `reapers`, the Grange's delegates on the granary terrace), the granary itself, the Syndicate's envoy at the
foot of the market terrace, the quay where barges land, and the golden grass at the foot of the hill, which in this contract is the uncut barley.

## The situation
Harvest week. The Reapers' Compact has laid down its scythes: the Crown's "royal bushel", the measure every reaper's day is paid by, is a third larger than the
bushel the Crown SELLS by (the reapers suspect it; nobody can prove it). The barley stands; the rain is coming. The Crown's Steward of the Granary wants the
harvest in and the Compact back at work by any means short of an inquiry. The Syndicate has a barge of strike-breakers ("seasonal labour, bonded") on the river
and a contract for the Crown's grain that pays it either way.

## Pressures (the party decides nothing from a menu; the ending is what these add up to)
- THE FOREPERSON of the Compact (parley `reaper`): states the demand; takes a bonus from the party's purse to end it (`pay` -> back to work, the grievance
  stands); agrees to an honest-measure settlement (`propose`) only once the fraud is PROVEN; or is told to go hang (`threaten` -> the strike turns ugly).
- THE ROYAL BUSHEL (a barrel prop at the granary's scale): carried to the Steward it is the proof (`use` with a barrel at the Steward). The Steward's own
  parley then offers the honest measure.
- THE STEWARD (parley `steward`): before the proof, offers the party a fee to "persuade" the Compact back (a `tip`: the strike-breakers come sooner, the
  Syndicate is pleased); after it, decrees an honest measure (`propose`).
- THE STRIKE-BREAKERS (cast group `late:breakers`, four rows, arriving by barge at `barge`, marching from the quay to the barley): if two reach the field
  before the Compact is back at work, the strike is broken. The party can turn them (fight: `hostile` at the group; or the Compact's own pickets fight them
  if the strike has turned ugly), or let them through.
- THE CLOCK: the rain (`rainAt`, seeded; the `rain` complication brings it forward). Barley standing in the rain is lost.

## Endings (first wins; the state freezes)
- `honest_measure`: the fraud proven to the Steward AND both the Steward and the Foreperson have agreed (in either order). The Compact goes back at the old
  rate on an honest bushel. Reapers' standing rises, the Crown is embarrassed, the Syndicate's contract shrinks.
- `bought_back`: the party paid the Foreperson's bonus. Work resumes; nothing is reformed; the party is poorer. Reapers mildly pleased; the Crown pleased.
- `strike_broken`: two strike-breakers reach the barley while the Compact is still out. The Syndicate gains; the Reapers' grudge against the Society and the
  Crown deepens.
- `barley_lost`: the rain arrives with the Compact still out. Nobody wins; grain prices rise; the Syndicate imports.
- `abandoned`: the party is down.

## Rules of the house (as every template)
Pure reducer over the existing event vocabulary; `observe` declares every id; roster <= 14 rows; four endings besides `abandoned`, materially different in
the campaign and the powers; leaving commits only what happened; weighted chaos from existing complication ids only (`rain`, `outriders`, `fog`).
Highmark's own picker weights the two templates; Kessar's weights are untouched (the backcompat digest).
