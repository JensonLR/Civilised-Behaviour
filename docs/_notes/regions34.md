## 2. The shape every region follows (C3 and D4 both)
- THE PLAN (`<r>.ts`): one pure description (anchors, authored things, `<r>Plan()`) that the terrain, the colliders, the props and the client geometry all read, like `kessarPlan`/`highmarkPlan`. The collision world is a pure function of (seed): deterministic over 5 seeds, true walls where a slope passes the step limit (no invisible walls), `resolveXZ` open at every `<r>SitePoints()`, every one reachable by a flood fill with the REAL `stepCharacter` on a 2 m grid from the landing (exceptions are named in the test with their reason), no anchor inside an obstacle, `buildNavGrid(world, <r>NavOptions(world))` connects landing to every site and builds in < 150 ms, four spawns and the `MOUNT_SPOTS` open, props open and deterministic. Anchor NUMBERS may be nudged <= 8 m (finals recorded in the file header); names and signatures may not.
- THE VIEW (`render/world/<r>/`): plan -> geometry per concern -> one view class (`<R>View implements RegionView`), toon-shaded, instanced, ink-outlined, palette colours only (a test rejects a literal), inside `<R>_VIEW_BUDGET` per preset (counted in Node), terrain painted from the plan, the day (`applyDay`), grass/reeds bending to walkers (`setPushers`), `dispose` frees everything (scene empty), `applyScenario` dresses the contract. Reuse `horizon.ts`, `water.ts`, `flora.ts`, `kit.ts`, `toon.ts`, `ambient.ts`: they are the game's; follow the pattern of `highmark/`, not its code.
- THE FAMILY: two templates, each a PURE reducer over the existing event vocabulary (`ScenarioInput`), `observe` declaring every id, `roster` <= 14 rows with unique ids (cast of both templates may not exceed `NPC_CAP` 24 together: a run spawns only its own template's cast), `outcome` carrying `region`, `leave` committing only what happened. >= 3 endings besides `abandoned`, MATERIALLY different: >= 3 distinct (campaign JSON, powers JSON) pairs that differ in >= 3 fields; decided by what the party did to each pressure (a person, a clock, a prop, a force), never by a menu; first resolution wins and the state freezes; a 5000-sequence hostile-event fuzz never throws and never commits twice; weighted chaos from EXISTING `ComplicationId`s only (`<R>_COMPLICATIONS`).
- THE LEDGER: one `EndingNumbers` row and one `EndingCopy` row per ending. `Rule` leaves the crossing, the toll and the bridge alone (`toll: "keep"`, `control: undefined`) and carries no `need`. `relations`: inside one template every pair of endings differs in >= 2 `PairKey`s; the home power's pairs carry the story. `minors`: the home power moves for EVERY ending; the paper's dispatch is `end_<resolution>` (>= 3 head and body variants). `memoryLine`/`siteLines` >= 2, headlines/standfirsts >= 3, `debrief` one plain line naming the place. `<R>_FAVOUR` may let the region's endings satisfy a pledged favour (the Guild is pleased by a sealed gallery, the Houses by a scuttled barge). `pick<R>Contract` weights its two templates from the ledger and `presence` WITHOUT touching Kessar's weights (`backcompat.test.ts` hashes them: digest `e36d8edd` unchanged).
- PARLEYS: scripts, not code (`ParleyScript`: speaker, `open` and `round2` >= 2 lines, `walk`, `hostile`, optional `flatter`, `deal` lines, `short`, `options(round, price)`; keys map to results: pay -> `paid`, propose -> `survey`, tell -> `tell`, envelope -> `envelope`, tip -> `tip`, ask -> second round + `learn`, flatter moves the price, threaten -> `hostile`); the template reads `talk {kind, result}`. A server never trusts the client: it re-derives options from (kind, round).
- TRAVEL AND CHART: flipping `reachable` puts the region on the chart, the map room's list and the travel machine; `campaignView` shows its note (`RegionCopy.chartNote` from `c.sites.ends` and `c.history`), the paper prints `end_<r>` dispatches and the ledger story under `<R>_STORY_HEADS`; sailing there from every shore works (every ordered pair already sails in the machine).
