# Polish pass 2: a game that looks, feels and sounds as good as it plays (D-038)

Goal: the player played it, and said: it feels good, the characters are funny, the music is jolly and everything LOOKS jolly (a fine contrast) but the grit and gore must be there too; the people we colonise must not look like us; the guns look broken and ugly; some cosmetics still overlap ugly on some shapes and the long hair is especially bad; third person is hard to aim (first person is great); the Xbox pad is fiddly and the UI never recognises it; the maps are beautiful but have broken ugly overlaps, broken doors and poor layout. And: NO Steamworks, no Playtest, no Demo page until the owner says; the game is to be in a VERY good state first (section 12).
Read with: `docs/LEVEL_PLAN.md` (binding, written for this pass), `docs/GDD.md` sections 3, 5, 6, 7; `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md`; `docs/BUILD_STATE.md` (every weak spot); `_notes/regions34.md`, `_notes/ship.md`, `_notes/fit.md`, `_notes/characters.md`, `_notes/combat.md`, `_notes/audio-ui.md`.
Verified by reading, not by looking: nobody has rendered this pass's problems. Every package starts by LOOKING (`node scripts/shot.mjs "<url>" out.png`, view it) and records what it saw. Software GL numbers are floors.

## 1. Diagnosis (cause, not symptom)
- **Maps.** Every authored building in Kessar, Highmark, Vesper and Saltmarket is ONE solid box (`box("house", ...)`) with a door, windows and steps DRAWN on it by the view: 27 buildings, 7 arches and the tents. Nothing is behind any door. Only Hollowmere's village has real doorways (`ringWalls`), and its cottages (1.15 m) and stilt houses (0.95 m) are narrower than a body wants. The Kessar fort is a sealed wall with a keep and halls inside it that are drawn but unreachable. Props are checked against walls but nothing checks buildings against each other: a flagpole stands inside the Customs House (0.88 m), a sign inside a spoil heap. Interiors are lit by nothing and roofs never cut away. The baseline is in LEVEL_PLAN section 9.
- **Aim in third person.** The server shoots from the HEAD (`COMBAT.eyeHeight` 1.55) along `aimYaw/aimElev`, which the client sets to the camera's yaw (`aimYawSlack` 0.6), while the camera sits behind and beside the head: at 8 m the round lands about 0.9 m from the crosshair. The aim camera widens the shoulder (1.15) and closes in (x0.62) but keeps the FOV, so targets stay small; no pad help; the reticle is not the spread.
- **Pad.** `Controls.ts` binds X to INTERACT and RELOAD at once (use a door, reload your gun); sprint is a stick click, view toggle an accidental R3 press, throw and grab on the bumpers; the layout is fixed and not rebindable; prompts hard-code "(X)" (`orientationCopy.ts`, `CombatHud.ts`, `controlsInfo.ts`); `usingGamepad` is a boolean (no PlayStation); `HowTo` detects a device once when it opens; `PadNav` handles menus but not every sheet's focus.
- **Gore does not persist.** `HitFx.ts` keeps 40 ground stains for 45 s and shrinks them to nothing; mud, soot and aftermath do not exist; wounds are dressings only. The jolly look needs its counterweight to STAY on the field.
- **Music** is one bed in two modes (`music.ts`: menu, game). Nothing responds to a fight, a death or a region.
- **Characters.** Everyone, villager or sentry, comes from one generator and the Victorian catalogue: natives are the Society in a different coat. Fit is audited (`fit.test.ts`) but on 2 shapes by default and one option at a time; combinations (hat x hair x neckwear x shoulder gear, long hair x collar x cape) are not swept. `Long Lank`, `Shaggy Mane`, `Plait` and `Ponytail` fall as shells from `hangProfile`; BUILD_STATE records "long hair reads as one cloth sheet from behind".
- **Guns** are built from axis-aligned boxes and cylinders (`WeaponModels.ts`, `PartBuilder`) around `WEAPON_ANCHORS` (`weaponPose.ts`); the viewmodel's rifle stock is a stub; the architect did not render them: W starts by looking.

Satire targets institutions; the Society's folk (players, hired hands, the Syndicate, deserters, the depot's own staff: `VILLAGER_PEOPLE` marks three trades colonial) KEEP the colonial caricature, so the contrast is the joke. ## 5. Input, aim and glyphs (package I)
- **Aim mode.** Hold LT / right mouse (or toggle: `holdToAim`): over-the-shoulder camera (`AIM.camera`: shoulder 0.95, distance x0.58, FOV x0.78, exponential blend `blendAim`, never snaps), reticle from the REAL spread (`reticleRadiusPx`), the character turns to the aim at the shared turn rate and walks at the server's `aimFactor` (0.7), look slows (mouse x0.72, pad `padAimSensitivity`). The shot comes from `aimSolve(eye, camPos, camDir, hitDist)`: `hitDist` is the first thing the crosshair ray meets (a march over the collision world and ground; `AIM.convergence.far` 45 m if nothing), so what is under the crosshair is what the round meets at any range. Clamped to the server's slack inside `aimSolve`; no wire change. Wall clearance for the aim camera (`AIM.camera.wallClearance`), and it never pulls inside the head.
- **Pad assist** (PAD ONLY, `getAimAssist`): `assistLook` over hostile NPC rows (the Cast's rows; `NpcBrain` modes), a cone (0.075 rad, larger for near bodies), pull as a RATE and look-stick slowdown, total drift under `AIM.assist.maxPull` (0.12 rad, a fifth of the slack). Mouse: never.
- **Pad v2** (`PROMPT_PAD` is the starting layout, tunable, names fixed): left stick move, right stick look, A jump, B crouch, X use (tap, only when something can be used) and reload (tap when nothing to use, always on hold), Y melee, LB throw, RB grab/drag, LT aim, RT fire, L3 sprint (hold; `holdToSprint` setting still applies), R3 HOLD (0.3 s) switch view (no accidental flips), d-pad up holster, left/right weapon, down HOLD command wheel, Start pause, View/Back skip. Radial deadzone `padDeadzone` (0.18) with a response curve `padCurve` exponent (1.6: fine near the centre, full at the rim), separate aim sensitivity, rumble (`padRumble`) on shots, hits and being hurt through `Gamepad.vibrationActuator` where present. Rebinding: a `PadBindings` table sanitised like `sanitizeBindings` (an action keeps a control; conflicts resolved by swap), saved with the same store, edited in a new `ui/SettingsPad.ts` section the integrator mounts in Settings.
- **Glyphs.** `deviceTracker.note(...)` from every input event (a key, mouse movement, a pad button or a stick past the deadzone; `padFamily(gamepad.id)` picks Xbox or PlayStation); `glyphs` setting pins one. EVERY prompt (HUD, how-to, sheets, parley, command wheel, orientation, pause, combat gauge, notices) is built with `promptText` / `glyphFor` and re-rendered on `deviceTracker.onChange`; a test scans `ui/` for hard-coded key names. Glyph elements are DOM (CSS shapes, no image assets): `.glyph-xbox-a`, `.glyph-ps-cross`, trigger, bumper, stick, d-pad.
- **Focus navigation for every sheet** by pad (`PadNav` extended): A activates, B closes (`padback`), LB/RB tabs (`padtab`), d-pad / stick move focus in reading order, the first focus is the primary action, a trapped sheet never loses focus; sliders step by the pad.
