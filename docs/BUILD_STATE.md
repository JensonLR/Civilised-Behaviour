## Lag compensation at 200 ms RTT (D-043; 2026-10-02)
**The defect.** `combatNet` "hitscan at 200 ms RTT" failed about one run in five (owner's count over ~19 runs on a 4-core container: 6..12 of 14 hits, mean ~0.7, bar 0.6). Reproduced here: 11, 7 and 7 of 14 in three runs.
**Root cause (measured, not inferred).** Every 200 ms shot needed 258..319 ms of rewind (28 shots, raw `renderTime` stamp vs `lastRecordedAt`) and the 250 ms clamp cut every one short; the rewound miss grew with the shortfall up to 0.4 m. A shot's lag is the WHOLE round trip plus the 100 ms display delay (the shot's own trip up was missing from the old "half RTT + delay" sum). The client's RTT estimate (224..252 ms at a simulated 200) cancels out of it and was not the cause. Detail: D-043.
